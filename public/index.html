What we cannot see

Two questions, not one

What happened? The five stories and the urgent list answer that. They rank events by seriousness, and each one needs two independent outlets. One dramatic event is not the same as sustained instability.

What is the state of the country? The level on each card and in the table answers that. It counts unique events weighted by seriousness, so a quiet stream of reports is not mistaken for nothing happening, and a flood of articles about one incident is not mistaken for a crisis.
